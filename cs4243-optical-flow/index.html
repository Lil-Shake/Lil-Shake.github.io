Single scale

large displacement → poor local linearization
Lucas–Kanade solves a local first-order brightness-constancy system. Starting from zero motion at full resolution can place the correct match outside the local basin of convergence, so large motions may be lost or estimated incorrectly.

Multi scale

16 px → 8 px → 4 px → 2 px
Downsampling shrinks apparent displacement. Motion is solved at a coarse level, doubled when propagated to the next finer level, and then refined. The pyramid-level slider now changes this computation directly.
What the sliders really change: pyramid levels control the number of coarse-to-fine refinements; LK window size changes the local normal-equation support; maximum corners changes how many Shi–Tomasi-like high-gradient points are tracked. Every control triggers a fresh optical-flow computation in your browser.

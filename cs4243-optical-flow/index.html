Single scale

large displacement → poor local linearization
At full resolution, Lucas–Kanade starts from zero motion. When the true match is far away, the local Taylor approximation cannot reach it reliably.

Multi scale

24 px → 12 px → 6 px → 3 px
Downsampling makes the same displacement smaller. The coarse estimate is propagated upward and refined at finer scales.
Try this in class: drag Added displacement stress from 0 toward 20–35 px. Both methods see exactly the same pair. The single-scale solver loses tracks first; pyramidal LK keeps more valid correspondences. The yellow vectors show tracks specifically recovered by the pyramid.
The animated input pair and ground-truth visualization come from the public MMFlow Sintel demo. The extra horizontal shift is an explicitly labeled teaching stress test, applied equally before comparing single-scale and multi-scale LK.

Single scale

large displacement → poor local linearization
The solver starts from zero displacement at full resolution. When the true match moves too far, the local Taylor approximation and patch overlap become unreliable.

Multi scale

16 px → 8 px → 4 px → 2 px
The same motion is smaller after downsampling. LK estimates a coarse displacement first, upsamples it, and refines the residual motion at progressively finer scales.
Try this in class: set Temporal gap = 1, then increase it toward 7–9. The gap deliberately increases motion between the compared frames. Watch the red missed points on the single-scale view and the yellow recovered vectors on the pyramidal view.

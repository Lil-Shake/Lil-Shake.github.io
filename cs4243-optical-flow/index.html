5 · Why the pyramid helps

Downsampling does not change the scene motion. It makes the same displacement smaller in pixel units, so local linearization becomes easier.
same motion · smaller at coarse scale
24 px → 12 px → 6 px → 3 px
Loading Sintel frames and computing the comparison…
Classroom takeaway: start at Extra motion = 0 px, then increase it toward 20–30 px. Single-scale LK should lose more correspondences as displacement grows. Pyramidal LK can still initialize the motion at a coarse level, where the same displacement is only a few pixels, and then refine it at finer levels.

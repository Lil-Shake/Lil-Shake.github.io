5 · Why the pyramid helps

Downsampling makes the same displacement smaller in pixel units, so the local linearization used by LK is easier to satisfy.
same motion · smaller at coarse scale
24 px → 12 px → 6 px → 3 px

Classroom takeaway: first play the MP4 to see the real sequence. Then set Extra motion = 0 px and increase it toward 20–30 px. Single-scale LK loses correspondences as displacement grows, while pyramidal LK can initialize motion at a coarse level and refine it at finer levels.
